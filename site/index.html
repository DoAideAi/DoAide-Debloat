Your phone should do what you paid for.

Ads on the lock screen, "hot apps" pushes, a service that profiles everything you do, three app stores. Point your coding agent at one repository and say clean up my phone. No root. Nothing uninstalled. One-line undo.

Get it on GitHub The prompt

Three steps, fifteen minutes

  1. On the phone: Settings › About phone › tap Build number seven times, then Developer options › USB debugging on. Plug it into your computer, or use Wireless debugging.
  2. On the computer: install adb, then
    git clone https://github.com/DoAideAi/DoAide-Debloat && cd DoAide-Debloat
    Open Claude Code, Codex, Hermes or Gemini CLI in that folder and say: Clean up my Samsung phone. Follow AGENTS.md.
  3. Answer one checklist: which of the optional apps (Samsung Pay, Bixby, Mi Video, Alexa…) you actually use. Only what you do not tick is turned off.

No agent? The same tools run by hand: probe, backup, plan, apply, restore. Python 3.9+ and adb, nothing else.

What it will not do

NeverRoot, remount, flash, factory reset, or delete anything from the system image.
Never touchesYour home screen, keyboard, phone, messages, browser and emergency apps, read live from the device; Play Services; account, security and update services.
AlwaysBacks up first, disables one package per command, re-checks the phone afterwards, and leaves a record file: restore --record <file> puts everything back.

Ran it on yours?

Send a device report: what the plan showed, what was refused, what you kept. The next person with your model skips the discovery.

What does your phone send home?

The X-ray records ten idle minutes of the phone's traffic before the clean-up and ten after, and turns the difference into a card you can share: how many ad and tracking servers it talked to. No root, nothing decrypted, and the recording stays on your computer.
